Lowercase, in SF Pro Rounded. The mark is drawn with a thin, even line, so the letters have to be as light as the line, or the mark looks weak next to them. Here are five weights at one size, with the mark beside each.

The rule
Master mark, Light weight. Wordmark size is 0.64 of the mark’s height. At 140 pt the mark’s line is about 5.8 and the letters about 5.9.
Small cut, Regular weight. Below 48 pt use the heavier mark with Regular letters, so the pair thickens together.
