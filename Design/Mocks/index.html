Psss interface mocks

Static mocks for owner approval, drawn on 23 Nov 2026. The first four pages below were approved on 23 Nov 2026; the AutoFill page is awaiting approval. Nothing here is built, and nothing in the app or docs has been changed to match. Each page shows every state side by side with a caption saying what the person sees and what they can do next, and ends with the decisions it embodies and where it departs from Apple's guidance.

How to read the frames. Plain HTML and CSS, system fonts, light and dark follow the system. Blue stands in for the system accent. Each device frame uses two text sizes, a title and the body, and every action has a text label. Controls are approximations of the native ones, not pixel-exact. iPad is described in words under each section, since it follows the Mac layout in a wide window and the iPhone layout in a narrow one. All data is fictional.
  • Recently Deleted: where it lives, the list, Restore and Delete Now, Delete All, the empty state, the two confirmations, a restore that fails, and delete without a confirmation but with Undo.
  • Item detail additions: Other fields, Attachments, Previous passwords with Restore and Clear, and the passive dates, with a full login, a bare one, after Restore, and the clear confirmation.
  • Import from 1Password 7: the whole flow, the review with changed secrets first, the clean case, partly unreadable, progress, done and deleting the export, seven errors, and a second import with Import These Too.
  • Conflict alert and lock notice: as the code has them today, with the wording I would change listed separately. The conflict alert is superseded by the new vault format: edits made on two devices no longer produce an alert. The lock notice still stands.
  • Create a vault and add a second device (approved 2026-11-23, not built): the first-run flow with no password and no recovery kit (the key is kept by the Apple Account, and the screen says what that means), a second device that simply has the vault, the fallbacks when the key does not arrive, and the errors (no iCloud, iCloud Keychain off, storage full, offline).
  • Locations, backup and self-hosting (approved 2026-11-23, not built): the vault's details with Advanced (vault password, recovery kit), adding a folder or a server, a backup copy that opens by itself or with a file password, restoring it, exporting as a KeePass file, groups in one vault, a sync with no conflict prompt, and a location that is unreachable, behind or removed.
  • Sharing with a family (approved 2026-11-23, not built): groups inside one vault (Only Me, Family, Sam's logins), moving or sharing an item, moving several items at once, creating a group, inviting a new person in person once and adding a known person with no code, a child, removing a person and the passwords to change, recovery pieces as an Advanced option, and the errors.
  • Turning on AutoFill (approved 2026-11-23, with one change: no separate step after the vault is created, the card is the onboarding): a calm reminder card at the top of the list while AutoFill is off, Setup showing the state, coming back from Settings, turned off later, and what Safari shows when nothing was done. The step in section 1 is drawn but not built.
  • Password generator (approved 2026-11-23, not built): the one card used in the item editor, the AutoFill sheet when a site asks for a new password, the Mac Safari popup and Setup. Revised after two owner reviews: Apple's strong-password shape by default (words are a type to choose), the password in its own field and hidden as a plain row of dots until Show with Copy always available, the strength hint beside the control that causes it, Customize reduced to a Type list, one Length slider (ticks for Words) and two switches (Include a number, Include a symbol) with the rejected stepper and Longer-switch alternatives drawn, the site's rules met without comment, a one-tap recovery when a site says no, and the fallback where the system sends no request. Plan: docs/plans/password-generator.md.
  • Tab bar and Codes (approved 2026-11-23, being built): an iPhone tab bar with Vault, Codes and the iOS 26 search tab; a Codes list where the code itself is the copy button, one countdown for the list, recently copied first; the copied, last-seconds, search, empty and broken-code states; Codes in the Mac and iPad sidebar; and which other tabs were considered and why they stay pills or actions. Plan: docs/plans/tab-bar-and-codes.md.
  • Command line and AI agents (proposed 2026-11-23, not approved, Mac only): the sheet a person sees when a program asks to use an item, by kind of use; the cases where it cannot go ahead; the Setup section with what is allowed and what happened; and what the terminal prints. Proposal: docs/cli.md.
